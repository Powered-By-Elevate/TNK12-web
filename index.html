Comprehensive development services

School development takes more than putting up a building.

True North K12 is the education-focused development branch of True North Companies. With experience across K–12 campuses, charter school facilities, early learning centers, and education-adjacent community projects, TNK12 understands that school development requires more than simply building a facility. As your trusted service partner, we are here to walk you through every step of the process.

01

Development services

Full development management, from the first feasibility conversation to the day the doors open.

02

Site selection

Finding and securing the right location, balancing accessibility, community need and room to grow.

03

Facility finance

Structuring what makes the campus possible, including grants and other funding strategies.

04

Design & build

Durable school design against real regulatory and budget limits, then built and managed to completion.

05

Lease or purchase

Occupancy structured the way the school needs it, whether that is a lease, a purchase, or a path between.
