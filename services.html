01 · Development services

Full development management, start to finish.

One accountable party across every discipline a campus touches. We run the program against the budget, hold the schedule, coordinate the consultants, and answer to the board for all of it. Most schools we work with have never built anything before, and they should not have to learn how in order to open on time.

As your trusted service partner, we are here to walk you through every step of the process, and to stay reachable long after the certificate of occupancy is issued.

What this covers

  • Feasibility studies and program validation
  • Budget and schedule modeling against enrollment
  • Entitlement and permitting strategy
  • Consultant selection and coordination
  • Owner representation through closeout
  • Authorizer and lender reporting support

02 · Site selection

The right land, secured before it costs you a year.

True North K-12 helps clients identify and secure the ideal location for new institutions, balancing accessibility, community need and long-term growth potential. The wrong parcel is the most expensive mistake available to a new school, and it is almost always made early, quietly, and for a good-sounding reason.

We look at what the site will cost to make buildable, not just what it costs to buy, and we plan the acreage for the school you intend to become rather than the one opening in year one.

What this covers

  • Enrollment-draw and market analysis
  • Site search, comparison and shortlisting
  • Zoning and entitlement due diligence
  • Civil, utility and topographic feasibility
  • Purchase, option and contract negotiation
  • Room reserved for a phase two

03 · Facility finance

Structuring what makes the campus possible.

True North K-12 helps clients secure the right financing options, including grants and other funding strategies, to bring the vision to life. Charter facilities are financed differently from almost anything else that gets built, and a capital stack assembled by someone who has not done it before tends to fail at the worst moment.

Our expertise spans the commercial real estate spectrum, finance through general contracting, which means the funding plan and the construction plan are built by people who are looking at the same numbers.

What this covers

  • Capital stack structuring
  • Charter-specific lending relationships
  • Bond and grant strategy
  • Pro forma modeling against the enrollment ramp
  • Developer-funded and landlord structures
  • Draw management and lender reporting

04 · Design & build

Designed for teaching, built to last, delivered on the calendar.

Together we create innovative and functional designs that enhance the learning experience while meeting every regulatory and budgetary requirement. Then we build them. From breaking ground to final inspections, we oversee every phase of construction so projects are completed on time and within budget.

A school calendar does not move. That single fact governs how we sequence a job, and it is why the schedule conversation starts at design rather than at the ribbon.

What this covers

  • Educational programming translated to square footage
  • Architect and engineer coordination
  • Code, life-safety and authorizer compliance
  • Value engineering before it becomes a change order
  • Pre-construction, GMP and construction management
  • Inspections, closeout and certificate of occupancy

05 · Lease or purchase options

Occupancy structured the way the school actually needs it.

Not every board wants to own a building on day one, and not every board should. We structure occupancy around the school's balance sheet and its charter term, whether that is a lease, a purchase, a turnkey developer-owned campus, or a path that starts as one and becomes the other.

True North K-12 also provides long-term maintenance strategies and ongoing support to ensure institutions remain operational and successful for years to come.

What this covers

  • Lease, purchase and lease-to-own paths
  • Turnkey developer-owned campuses
  • Terms written against the charter term
  • Expansion and phase-two options preserved
  • Long-term maintenance planning
  • Post-construction support and warranty

How it goes

Six phases, and you know where you are in all of them.

Every campus is different and every one of them runs this sequence. The value is not the diagram, it is that nobody on your board has to guess what happens next or who owns it.

PHASE 01

Feasibility

Enrollment projections, program, order-of-magnitude budget and an honest read on whether the target opening is real.

PHASE 02

Site

Search, diligence and control of the land, with zoning and civil answered before money is committed.

PHASE 03

Capital

The stack assembled and closed, grants and lenders lined up against a pro forma the authorizer can read.

PHASE 04

Design

Architect and engineers coordinated to the program, priced continuously, permitted to build.

PHASE 05

Construction

Ground broken and managed to a school calendar, with the board seeing the same schedule we do.

PHASE 06

Occupancy

Inspections, certificate of occupancy, furniture and keys, then maintenance strategy and a phone that still gets answered.
