Our mission

Our mission and values are the compass that guides everything we do, acting as our true north in every project.

At True North K-12 we view charter schools as vital pillars of their communities, not just centers for education. They play a crucial role in driving economic growth and fostering lifelong success for young Americans.

Our goal is to assist authorizers in creating outstanding charter schools built on the age-old principles of trust, respect and diligence, essential for empowering the next generation to thrive and make positive contributions to society. By focusing on innovative school development, we strive to create environments that inspire long-term growth, resilience and a lasting commitment to learning.

From the grandest vision to the smallest task, each action we take is rooted in our commitment to innovation, excellence and community building.

Part of True North Companies

A development company that happens to love schools.

True North Companies is a privately held real estate solutions company dedicated to building places where people belong. Since 2013 its purpose-driven ethos has driven transformative initiatives that leave a lasting legacy for future generations, across education, mixed-use, senior living, office, hospitality, retail and multifamily.

True North K-12 is the branch that does nothing but education. It means a charter board gets a team that has built schools before, backed by a company with the balance sheet and the trades to finish them.

  • Founded 2013. True North K-12 established 2024.
  • Over six decades of combined real estate expertise
  • Over 30 years combined in the education sector
  • Finance through general contracting under one roof
  • Headquartered in Spartanburg, South Carolina
